Your debts

Started at is what you owed when you began tracking — set it once and leave it. Owing now counts every payment you've ticked in This cycle, across all cycles, so it keeps moving on its own. Paid something straight from your bank instead? Click Owing now and put in the real figure. Rate is annual interest; leave it at 0 for interest-free. Clear by is that debt on its own minimum. The big Clear by at the top runs the whole plan together: interest accrues, spare money goes to your highest-rate debt first, and a cleared debt's payment rolls onto the next one.
