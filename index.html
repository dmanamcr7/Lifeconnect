Welcome to Connected Life

One app for training, money and the house — and a page that reads all three together.

Where to start
  1. Money → Setup · a payday you know, how long your pay cycle is, your income and bills. Five minutes, and every other number in the app comes from it.
  2. Groceries → At home · add what you have in the house. Mark anything you can't run out of as critical — when it hits zero it goes on the list by itself.
  3. Gym → Train · add an exercise and log your first set. The app works out your working weight and what to do next.

Connected fills itself in once there's a gym bill in Money and a few sessions in Gym.

Everything is saved on this device. Use the sync button in the header to carry Gym and Money to another phone; Groceries has its own household sharing. This page is always under the ? at the top.

Your debts

Started at is what you owed when you began tracking — set it once and leave it. Owing now counts every payment you've ticked in This cycle, across all cycles, so it keeps moving on its own. Paid something straight from your bank instead? Click Owing now and put in the real figure. Rate is annual interest; leave it at 0 for interest-free. Clear by is that debt on its own minimum. The big Clear by at the top runs the whole plan together: interest accrues, spare money goes to your highest-rate debt first, and a cleared debt's payment rolls onto the next one.
